About X-Tra

We bring function, comfort and character together.

X-Tra is an interior design company focused on creating practical, modern spaces. We work across interior design, home decoration, exterior concepts and kitchen design.

Every project begins with the people who will use the space. We then connect layout, lighting, materials, furniture and decoration into one clear design that feels personal and easy to live with.
