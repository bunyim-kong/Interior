Questions & answers

What would you like to know?

What design areas does X-Tra cover?

The site explores interior design, home decoration, exterior design and kitchen design. You can see an overview on the Services page.

Where can I see project examples?

The Project page collects the images and design directions featured throughout this website. You can filter the gallery by interiors, kitchens and details.

Can I ask about a specific room?

Yes. Use the Contact page to describe the room, what you want to change and any ideas you already have.

How should I prepare for a design conversation?

A few photos, rough measurements and examples of styles you like are a great start. It also helps to explain how you use the space every day.

How do I ask about an item in the shop?

The Shop page shows decorative ideas from the original room scene. Use its email enquiry links to ask about a particular item; checkout is not available on this static site.

Is this website usable on phones and tablets?

Yes. The navigation, gallery, cards and forms adapt to smaller screens, and the menu can be used with a keyboard.
